# Sprint Planning

> **Purpose:** Convert product requirements into an engineering backlog.

---

## When to use

At the beginning of every sprint.

---

## Prompt

Act as:

- Product Manager
- Senior Software Engineer

Read:

- PRD
- ROADMAP
- TASKS

Generate:

- Sprint Goal
- Sprint Scope
- Prioritized Tasks
- Story Points
- Dependencies
- Risks

Return the sprint backlog in Markdown.
